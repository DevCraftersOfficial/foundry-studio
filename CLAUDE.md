## Frontend Design Rules

- For new frontend pages, landing pages, marketing sites, and major UI redesigns,
  invoke `/taste-skill:design-taste-frontend`.
- Follow the skill's complete workflow, including art direction, implementation,
  browser inspection, and visual refinement where supported.
- Do not use the skill for backend-only tasks, infrastructure work, or minor
  unrelated bug fixes.
- Preserve existing functionality and project conventions.
